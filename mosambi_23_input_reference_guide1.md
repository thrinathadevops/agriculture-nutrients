## 06 | WAE — Wood Ash Extract (Neutralized)

**Type:** Homemade | **Prep time:** 48 hours settle + neutralize | **Shelf life:** Use within 24 hours of neutralizing

### Preparation
```
2 kg wood ash (neem/pongamia/mango/coconut shell — best types)
+ 20L water → stir 5 min → cover → settle 48 hours undisturbed
Take ONLY top clear liquid (mother solution) — discard grey sludge

BEFORE EVERY USE — NEUTRALIZE:
Take 3L mother solution + 3L MOE → mix → check pH → must be 6.5–7.5
If above 7.5 → add more MOE → retest
Never use without neutralizing — raw WAE at pH 9–11 damages roots and leaves
```

### Key Nutrients and Compounds
| Component | Level | Benefit |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Boron (B)** | 🔴 HIGH — CLOSES GAP | Pollen tube germination + sugar transport leaf→fruit |
| **Manganese (Mn)** | 🟠 Moderate | Photosynthesis enzyme (Mn-SOD) — keeps leaves green |
| Potassium (K) | 🔴 Very High | 3–7% in ash — highest K of all natural inputs |
| Calcium (Ca) | 🔴 Very High | 20–30% as CaCO3 — massive source |
| Magnesium (Mg) | 🟡 Moderate | Chlorophyll support |
| Silica (Si) | 🟡 Moderate | Cell wall hardening |

### Application
| Route | Dose per 100L | Timing |
|-------|--------------|--------|
| Spray | 3L neutralized solution + 97L water | On leaves. On buds (Period 2 — before flowers open) |
| Drip | 1L neutralized per 100L | Only after mixing with MOE — test pH before opening drip |

### Best Periods
**Most Critical:** P2 (Boron for pollen tubes), P12–P14 (Boron for sugar loading)
**Active:** Every odd period — P1, P3, P5, P7... alternating spray role
**Avoid:** Never exceed 3% spray. Never drip above pH 7.0

### Unique Benefit
```
ONLY natural source of significant Boron in your system

Without Boron in P12+: sugar cannot move from leaf to fruit → low Brix
Boron is phloem mobile — absorbs via leaf and goes everywhere in plant
Neutralizing with MOE also gives pH correction benefit to the mixed solution
```

### Do Not Mix With
