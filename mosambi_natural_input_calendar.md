```

pH check     : Test header tank water pH before opening drip lines

VAM drip     : Water field lightly before VAM → apply VAM drip → water again after
              → Pushes spores to root zone depth
Banana/BSJ   : Flush lines extra 20 minutes after — sugar clogs emitters
```

# APPENDIX A — EMERGENCY RESPONSE GUIDE

## Emergency 1 — Excessive Fruit Drop (>80% in Period 3–5)

```
Immediate action:
Day 1 → CNW 10% spray morning + evening
Day 2 → EJEV 10% spray + BTM 3% directly on fruitlets
Day 3 → CNW 10% spray again
Day 4 → WAE 3% spray (Boron)
Continue every 3 days until drop stabilizes
```

## Emergency 2 — Leaf Yellowing (Iron Deficiency at pH 8.2)

```
Immediate action:
Day 1 → FAA 5% spray (double dose) morning
Day 2 → BSJ 5% spray
Day 3 → FAA 5% spray again
Every 7 days until color returns to dark green
Long term: VAM drip to unlock soil Fe
Do NOT drip iron sulfate — locks in your alkaline soil
```

## Emergency 3 — Gummosis / Trunk Oozing

```
Immediate action:
Day 1 → Scrape affected area gently
         Apply TRCH concentrate paste directly on wound
Day 2 → TRCH 10% trunk drench around base
Day 3 → NWDC 5% drip in root zone
Day 7 → Second TRCH trunk drench
Monthly → Continue TRCH trunk drench until gum stops
```

## Emergency 4 — Pest Attack (Mealybug / Thrips / Aphids)

```
Immediate action:
Day 1 → LRK 5% spray (double concentration) morning
Day 2 → GGE 5% spray evening
Day 3 → LRK 5% spray
Repeat every 5 days until pest population drops
Never mix LRK and GGE in same tank
```

## Emergency 5 — Heat Stress / Wilting Leaves

```
Immediate action:
Day 1 → CNW 10% spray morning 6 AM
         Plain water spray evening 5 PM
Day 2 → PCG 3% spray morning
Day 3 → CNW 10% spray again
Continue until leaves recover turgor
Ensure drip emitters are not blocked
```

## Emergency 6 — pH of Drip Water Still Above 7.5

```
Action:
→ Add extra MOE to header tank
→ Test pH every 10 minutes until 6.0–6.5
→ If still not dropping → add BTM (buttermilk) to tank
→ MOE + BTM combined are very acidic
→ Never proceed with drip above pH 7.0
→ Long term: increase tilapia count in sump → more CO2 → lower pH naturally
```
