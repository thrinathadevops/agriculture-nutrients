## Bahar Induction — Pre-Season Stress Protocol

### This is MANDATORY for pomegranate — different from citrus

```
STEP 1 — WATER STRESS (45–60 DAYS before intended flowering):
→ Completely STOP irrigation
→ Allow soil to dry — leaves show slight wilting = correct signal
→ Continue stress for 45–60 days
→ This forces tree into dormancy → triggers uniform bud break

STEP 2 — STRESS PERIOD INPUTS (every 15 days during stress):
→ Light Jeevamrutha drip ONLY: JEV 10% in 100L — once in 15 days
→ Purpose: Keep soil biology alive — do not feed tree
→ TRCH trunk drench 10%: Once during stress period
→ NO sprays during stress period — tree is dormant

STEP 3 — PRUNING (during stress period — Day 20–30 of stress):
→ Remove all dead, diseased, crossing branches
→ Remove suckers from rootstock
→ Remove water shoots
→ Cut back to 2–3 healthy lateral branches per shoot
→ Immediately after pruning: Paint cuts with TRCH 10% paste
→ This prevents fungal entry at pruning wounds

STEP 4 — END OF STRESS → START IRRIGATION (Day 1 of Calendar):
→ Resume irrigation
→ First irrigation: Add TRCH 5% in header tank
→ This is Day 1 of your 14-period calendar
→ Tree will flush within 7–10 days = Period 1 begins
```

# SECTION 2 — DISEASE AND PEST CONTROL COVERAGE

## What Controls Each Major Disease/Pest

---

### 1. BACTERIAL BLIGHT (Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. punicae)
**Most devastating disease — dark brown/black lesions on leaves, stem, fruit**

```
PREVENTION CHAIN:
→ ALV spray (Aloe Vera): Acemannan + anthraquinones = strongest natural bactericide
→ TRM spray (Turmeric): Curcumin directly kills Xanthomonas — spray every 2 periods
→ GGE spray (Garlic+Ginger): Allicin — broad spectrum antibacterial
→ BTM spray: LAB colonies compete with and suppress Xanthomonas on leaf surface
→ LRK drip: Tannins + Nimbin — antifungal and antibacterial in soil

COMBINED COVERAGE: ALV + TRM + GGE + BTM = complete bacterial blight prevention
None of the 23 Mosambi inputs alone covers blight — these 2 new inputs (ALV + TRM) are critical
```

---

### 2. FUSARIUM WILT (Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. punicae)
**Sudden wilting and death of branches/whole tree — most feared disease**

```
PREVENTION AND CONTROL CHAIN:
→ TRCH drip (Trichoderma): Attacks Fusarium in soil — already in system
→ PLF drip (Pseudomonas fluorescens): NEW — strongest Fusarium biocontrol known
   Produces 2,4-DAPG compound that specifically kills Fusarium
→ PKA drip (Panchakashtha): Azotobacterin antifungal + neem nimbin
→ VAM: Mycorrhizal network makes root zone hostile to Fusarium
→ GRK (Gro Kupha): EPIB Bacillus subtilis — documented Fusarium suppressor

WILT EMERGENCY TREATMENT (if tree shows wilting):
→ Remove affected branch immediately
→ TRCH 10% paste on cut surface
→ PLF 10% drip around entire root zone
→ TRCH 10% drench → daily for 5 days
→ GRK 5% drip next day
→ Repeat TRCH + PLF alternating every 3 days until tree recovers

COMBINED COVERAGE: PLF + TRCH + VAM + PKA = comprehensive wilt control
PLF (Pseudomonas) is the critical new addition that was missing in Mosambi system
```

---

### 3. NEMATODES (Root knot — Meloidogyne incognita)
**Root galls destroy feeding roots — tree looks nutrient deficient**

```
EXISTING 23 INPUTS ALREADY COVER THIS:
→ NWDC (Neem Waste): Azadirachtin disrupts nematode egg hatching — every period
→ GRK (Karanjin): Contact nematicide — root zone soil
→ PKA (Ricinoleic acid from castor): Membrane disruptor — Mechanism 4
→ PKA (Neem in 5 cakes): Azadirachtin — Mechanism 1
→ VAM: Mycorrhizal root protection — nematodes cannot penetrate VAM-colonized roots
→ FAA (Fish Amino Acid): Fatty acids that improve root membrane integrity

COMBINED COVERAGE: All 5 mechanisms active in root zone simultaneously
No new input needed for nematode — existing system covers well
Apply NWDC + GRK consistently every period in drip for continuous control
```

---

### 4. NALLI — SMALL WHITE INSECTS (Thrips + Aphids + Mealybug)

```
IDENTIFICATION:
→ White tiny insects on tender shoots = THRIPS (Thrips parvispinus)
→ Green/black clusters on tips = APHIDS (Aphis punicae)
→ White cottony masses on branches = MEALYBUG (Phenacoccus solenopsis)
→ ALL THREE are called "Nalli" commonly in Karnataka/Andhra

EXISTING 23 INPUTS COVER ALL THREE:
→ LRK spray 3%: Annonacin (sheetapal) = most effective against sucking insects
   Azadirachtin (neem) = disrupts pest hormone cycle
   → MOST IMPORTANT spray for Nalli control
→ GGE spray 3%: Allicin repellent — pests avoid treated plants
→ PKA spray 3%: Neem azadirachtin + karanjin + ricinoleic acid
→ NWDC spray 5%: Neem compounds
→ PLF (new): Pseudomonas secretes compounds toxic to many insects

COMBINED COVERAGE: LRK + GGE + PKA = comprehensive Nalli control
No new input needed — spray LRK when first Nalli seen (do not wait)
If severe: Double LRK to 5% spray + GGE 5% next morning
```

---

### 5. FRUIT BORER (Deudorix isocrates / Virachola isocrates)
**Butterfly larvae bore into fruit — fruit drops prematurely — major loss**

```
LIFE CYCLE UNDERSTANDING:
→ Adult butterfly lays eggs on flower/small fruit surface
→ Larvae bore into calyx and enter fruit
→ One larvae destroys entire fruit from inside
→ Critical window: Egg laying at flowering and small fruit stage P3–P5

CONTROL CHAIN:
→ LRK spray 3%: Azadirachtin disrupts adult butterfly egg laying behavior
   Spray specifically in evening = when butterflies are active
→ PKA spray 3%: Neem compounds repel adult butterfly
→ KLN spray (Kaolin clay): PHYSICAL BARRIER — most effective mechanical defense
   Clay particle layer on fruit surface = butterfly cannot find laying site
   No chemistry — pure physical protection
→ Pheromone traps: Install at 1 trap per 30 trees = catches adult male butterfly

COMBINED COVERAGE: LRK + PKA (repel) + KLN (physical barrier) = comprehensive borer control
KLN (Kaolin clay) is the critical new addition for borer
Start KLN from when fruit reaches marble size (P5–P6 onwards)
```

## NEW INPUT 24 | ALV — Aloe Vera Extract
**CRITICAL for Bacterial Blight — most important new addition**

### Preparation
```
Fresh aloe vera leaves → peel outer skin → collect inner gel
Blend or crush → mix with equal volume water
Ferment 2–3 days (mild fermentation — slight bubbling)
OR: Use fresh (no fermentation) — both work
Ready: Slight sour smell + gel consistency reduced + clear liquid
Strain through cloth — remove solid pieces
Make fresh every 5 days

Alternative: Commercial aloe vera extract (concentrated) — dilute as per label
```

### Key Compounds
| Compound | Level | Benefit |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Acemannan | 🔴 High | Antibacterial + antiviral — inactivates Xanthomonas |
| Anthraquinones | 🔴 High | Aloin, emodin — direct bacterial cell destruction |
| Enzymes | 🟠 High | Catalase, amylase — boost plant immunity |
| Saponins | 🟠 High | Natural surfactant — helps spray stick + antibacterial |
| Salicylic acid | 🟡 Moderate | SAR trigger — plant immune system |
| Vitamins C+E | 🟡 Moderate | Antioxidant protection |

### Application

### Best Periods
**ALL periods P1–P14:** Include in every spray without exception

## NEW INPUT 25 | TRM — Turmeric + Lime Extract
**Specific bactericide for Xanthomonas blight**

### Preparation
```
Turmeric (Haldi) powder: 200g
Hydrated lime (Chuna): 50g
Warm water: 5 liters
→ Dissolve turmeric in warm water
→ Add lime (raises pH briefly — curcumin more stable at alkaline pH)
→ Mix well → let steep 24 hours
→ Filter through cloth
→ Working extract ready — no fermentation needed
→ Neutralize before drip: test pH → if above 7.5 → add MOE
→ For spray: check pH 6.5–7.5 after 24 hours
```

### Key Compounds
| Compound | Level | Benefit |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Curcumin | 🔴 Very High | Directly inhibits Xanthomonas cell wall synthesis |
| Turmerone | 🟠 High | Antifungal — broad spectrum |

## NEW INPUT 26 | PLF — Pseudomonas fluorescens Liquid

**Critical for Fusarium Wilt prevention**

### What It Is
```
Pseudomonas fluorescens = gram-negative soil bacterium
Commercial biological product — available as liquid inoculant
Most researched biocontrol agent for Fusarium wilt worldwide

How it works:
→ Produces 2,4-Diacetylphloroglucinol (2,4-DAPG) compound
→ 2,4-DAPG specifically inhibits Fusarium oxysporum germination
→ Colonizes root surface — creates protective zone
→ Produces HCN — antifungal compound
→ Induces ISR (Induced Systemic Resistance) in tree

Pseudomonas + Trichoderma together:
→ TRCH attacks Fusarium mycelium (body of fungus)
→ PLF prevents Fusarium spore germination
→ Together they cover complete Fusarium life cycle
→ This combination is recommended by NRCP (National Research Centre on Pomegranate)
```

### Application
| Route | Dose per 100L | Timing |
|-------|--------------|--------|
| Spray | 5L PLF + 95L water | Trunk and root zone spray |
| Drip | 3L PLF + 97L water | Root zone colonization — every period |
| Soil drench | 10% concentration | Emergency wilt treatment |

### Best Periods
**ALL periods P1–P12:** Include in Drip 4 every period (NWDC + PLF + VMW)
**Pre-season:** Apply during stress period soil drench for baseline protection

### Do Not Mix With
```
Compatible with TRCH in drip (different kingdom — bacteria vs fungi)
Do not apply same day as high-acid inputs (MOE, BTM) — 2-day gap
Give 2-day gap from WAE drip
```

## NEW INPUT 27 | KLN — Kaolin Clay Spray
**Physical barrier for Fruit Borer and Thrips**

### What It Is
```
Kaolin clay (Particle Film Technology)
Fine white clay powder mixed with water
Creates physical film on fruit and leaf surface
PURELY MECHANICAL — no chemistry — zero residue concerns

How it works:
→ Clay particles deposit on fruit surface when dry
→ Creates white film barrier
→ Fruit borer butterfly CANNOT find egg-laying site through film
→ Thrips cannot grip clay-coated surface — falls off
→ Does not enter plant — purely external barrier
→ Washes off after harvest — zero residue

Commercial kaolin: Surround WP or similar available in India
OR: Local china clay (processed to fine particle — similar effect)
```

### Application
| Route | Dose per 100L | Timing |
|-------|--------------|--------|
| Spray on fruit | 30–50g KLN per litre water | 300–500g per 100L |
| NOT for drip | ❌ Clay particles block emitters | — |

### Best Periods
**P5 onwards** (when fruit reaches marble size — 2–3 cm)
**P5–P13:** Apply to fruit surface every spray day
**Reapply after rain:** Rain washes off clay — reapply within 24 hours of rain

### Key Instructions
```
Mix: 300–500g kaolin per 100L water
Stir continuously while spraying — clay settles fast
Spray early morning — better adhesion when humidity lower
Target: Coat all fruit surfaces visible — uniform white film
After heavy rain: Reapply immediately
Do not apply at flowering — blocks stomata on petals
```

# DISEASE EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

## Emergency 1 — Bacterial Blight Active (Lesions Visible)

```
Day 1 Morning: ALV 10% + TRM 5% + GGE 5% spray on all affected parts
Day 1 Afternoon: Apply ALV gel (undiluted) paste on each lesion directly
Day 2 Morning: BTM 5% spray on all leaves
Day 2 Drip: PLF 5% + VMW 10% drip
Day 3 Morning: TRM 5% + ALV 10% spray again
Day 4: TRCH 10% trunk drench
Repeat ALV + TRM spray every 2 days until lesions stop spreading

Prevention after rain: ALWAYS spray ALV 5% + TRM 3% within 24 hours of any rainfall
```

## Emergency 2 — Fusarium Wilt (Branch Wilting Suddenly)

```
Day 1: Cut wilting branch 30cm below wilting point
Apply TRCH 10% paste on cut surface immediately
PLF 10% soil drench entire root zone — 10L per tree
Day 2: TRCH 5% drip + PLF 5% drip
Day 3: GRK 5% drip (EPIB bacteria reinforce)
Day 5: VAM 5% drip (rebuild root health)
Day 7: TRCH 10% drench again
Continue TRCH + PLF alternating drip every 3 days for 30 days
If whole tree wilts: Difficult to save. Remove tree, treat soil with TRCH+PLF for 30 days before replanting
```

## Emergency 3 — Nalli (Heavy Aphid/Thrips/Mealybug Infestation)

```
Day 1 Morning: LRK 5% (double dose) spray entire tree
Day 1 Evening: GGE 5% + ALV 5% spray
Day 2 Morning: PKA 4% spray
Day 3 Morning: LRK 5% again
Day 4 Evening: GGE 5% again
Continue LRK every 5 days until population zero
DRIP: NWDC 5% + PLF 3% drip simultaneously for soil stage control
Check: If mealybug specific → add liquid soap 2ml per litre to LRK spray (breaks wax coat)
```

## Emergency 4 — Fruit Borer (Bored Fruit Found on Tree)

```
Immediate: Remove and destroy ALL bored fruit — do not leave on ground
Spray: LRK 5% on all remaining fruit — evening spray
Same day: Apply KLN 500g per 100L on all remaining fruit — double coat
Tomorrow: PKA 4% spray on all fruit
Check pheromone traps: If >8 moths per week — emergency LRK spray every 3 days
Prevention ongoing: Never let KLN barrier lapse — reapply after every rain
```

## Emergency 5 — Fruit Cracking (Major Risk in Pomegranate)

```
Cause: Uneven water + Ca deficiency during rapid enlargement
Prevention (P7–P10):
→ NEVER stop irrigation suddenly — gradual increase/decrease only
→ BTM spray on fruit surface every evening (P7–P11)
→ Increase MOE to 2.5% in drip — K regulates water movement
→ WAE spray every spray day — Si + Ca in peel
→ BSJ spray every spray day during P8–P10 — Si hardens peel

If cracking starts:
→ BTM 5% spray on cracked fruit immediately
→ KLN 500g per 100L on all intact fruit — barrier protects
→ Reduce irrigation volume for 3 days → resume gradually
→ Increase Ca via drip: BTM 1.5% in drip for 2 sessions
```
