# SCHEDULE 3B — LOW-COST / HALF-PRICE VERSION

**Goal:** run the same two-bahar cycle but cut cost to about **half**, by replacing bought chemicals with the **homemade inputs you already make**, or by using **half chemical + half homemade**. This is a *separate* file — your full Schedule 3 stays as it is.

**The honest rule first:** a few inputs **cannot** be replaced on your pH 8.3 salty soil — replacing them = lost crop. Those are marked **KEEP** below. Everything else can be cut, halved, or swapped.

---

> To reach a true **50% (≈₹39,000)**: use **family labour** for all homemade inputs (FAG, ELE, Jeevamrutha, neem, EM/Tricho multiplication) and grow green manure aggressively in both rest windows. The chemicals are the small part — your savings come mostly from **green manure replacing FYM/oil cake** and **homemade replacing bought organics**.

---

## DO NOT CUT THESE (or you lose crop)

- **Boron, Zinc, Fe-EDDHA** — your soil is genuinely deficient; homemade cannot supply them. (Use cheap **Borax**, **half Zn** with VAM/EM, Fe-EDDHA **only when pale** — that already lowers cost without dropping them.)
- **MKP at flowering, SOP at fruit** — reduce with banana juice, but keep the core dose; they drive flowering and size.
- **Gypsum (once/yr) + MOE (every drip)** — these fight your pH 8.3 + salt. Cutting them undoes everything else.
- **Nitrobenzene + GA3** — tiny cost, no homemade equal; keep.

**What actually makes it cheap:** green manure (sunhemp/daincha) in the June and Oct-Nov rest windows replaces most of the FYM + oil cake, and homemade FAG / ELE / vermiwash / neem / multiplied biologicals replace the bought organics — for the price of seed and your own labour.
