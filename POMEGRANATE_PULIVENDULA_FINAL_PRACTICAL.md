# 🌟 TNAM — CHINTA NEERRU COMPLETE PREPARATION GUIDE
## Most Important Input for Pulivendula Black Soil + Beluku Conditions

## Why TNAM is Critical for Your Farm

```
YOUR PROBLEM:
→ Black cotton soil natural pH: 7.5–8.0
→ Beluku (lime/dolomite) application: Raises pH to 8.5–9.0
→ Borewell water pH: 8.2
→ Combined soil pH: Approximately 8.5–9.0

AT pH 8.5–9.0:
→ Iron (Fe): Completely locked → leaf yellowing
→ Zinc (Zn): Completely locked → small fruit, poor size
→ Phosphorus: 80% locked as calcium phosphate
→ Manganese: Locked → pale leaves
→ Only Ca and Mg: Too much (from beluku itself)

TNAM SOLUTION:
→ Tartaric acid (main acid in tamarind) = pH 2.9
→ Strongest natural acidifier available locally
→ Tartaric acid also CHELATES locked minerals
→ Chelation = tartaric acid grabs Fe, Zn, Mn
→ Forms tartrate complexes that plant can absorb
→ WORKS EVEN AT pH 9.0 — chemical chelates work similarly
→ Locally available, zero cost in Pulivendula area
```

## TNAM Preparation — Step by Step

### Method 1 — Quick (24 hours)
```
Materials:
→ Fresh or dry tamarind (Chinta): 2 kg
→ Water: 20 liters

Process:
Step 1 → Take 2 kg tamarind (kinda/chintapanda)
          No need to remove seeds or outer shell
Step 2 → Add 20 liters water in a bucket or vessel
Step 3 → Submerge tamarind fully in water
Step 4 → Cover with cloth
Step 5 → Wait 24 hours minimum
Step 6 → During soaking, squeeze and crush the pods 3-4 times
          This releases more tartaric acid
Step 7 → After 24 hours, squeeze and press pods thoroughly
Step 8 → Strain through cloth — remove fibres and seeds
Step 9 → You get approximately 15–18 liters of TNAM extract

Result:
→ Color: Dark brown liquid
→ Smell: Sour, characteristic tamarind smell
→ pH: Approximately 3.0–3.5
→ Use within 5 days
→ Store in shade
```

### Method 2 — Fermented (3–5 days, more potent)
```
Same materials and process as Method 1
BUT:
→ After 24 hours of soaking, leave 3-4 more days to ferment
→ Stir once daily
→ Light foaming will appear = lactic acid bacteria developing
→ Ready when: Stronger sour smell + light foam + color deepens
→ More potent than Method 1 — preferred for drip pH correction
→ Also develops LAB for additional disease control benefit
→ Use within 10 days of preparation
```

### Larger Batch for Farm Use
```
For 200L preparation drum (large batch):
→ 20 kg tamarind + 200 liters water
→ Same soaking process for 24–72 hours
→ Squeeze well through cloth
→ You get approximately 150–170 liters TNAM
→ This is your TNAM preparation drum
→ Store in shade, use within 7 days

For 100L batch:
→ 10 kg tamarind + 100 liters water
→ Follow same process
```

# NEW INPUTS — PREPARATION AND DOSES

## ALV — Aloe Vera Extract
```
PREPARATION:
→ Fresh aloe vera leaves → remove outer green skin → collect inner gel
→ Grind or crush gel → mix with equal water
→ Ferment 2-3 days (optional — fresh also works)
→ Strain through cloth

DOSE:
→ Spray 1000L: 50L ALV → fill to 1000L
→ Drip 200L drum: 4L ALV → fill to 200L
→ ADD TO EVERY SPRAY SESSION — always
→ Also add to Drip Session 2 (Day 6) every period
```

## TRM — Turmeric + Lime Extract
```
PREPARATION:
→ Turmeric (Haldi) powder: 2 kg
→ Hydrated lime (Chuna): 500g
→ Water: 50 liters
→ Dissolve turmeric in warm water
→ Add lime, stir well
→ Steep 24 hours, strain
→ Test pH: Should be 6.5–7.5 after 24 hrs
→ If above 7.5, add 2L MOE and retest

DOSE:
→ Spray 1000L: 30L TRM → fill to 1000L
→ Add to evening spray (GGE + ALV + TRM together)
→ Use every 2 periods for blight prevention
→ After any rain event: Spray TRM within 24 hours
```

## PLF — Pseudomonas fluorescens
```
PREPARATION:
→ Commercial biological product (liquid inoculant)
→ Available from agriculture shops
→ Add to water as per label
→ Use within 5 days of opening

DOSE:
→ Spray 1000L: 50L PLF → fill to 1000L
→ Drip 200L drum: 6L PLF → fill to 200L
→ Goes in DRIP 4 and DRIP 5 every period
→ Do not mix with acidic inputs same day (MOE/BTM)
→ Give 2-day gap from WAE in drip
```

## KLN — Kaolin Clay
```
PREPARATION:
→ Commercial kaolin clay (Surround WP or china clay)
→ Mix: 300-500g per 100L water (for spray tank = 3-5kg per 1000L)
→ Stir continuously — clay settles fast
→ Spray on fruit surfaces only

DOSE:
→ Spray 1000L: 3–5 kg KLN stirred in 1000L water
→ Apply as SEPARATE spray on FRUIT SURFACE ONLY
→ Do on same day as morning spray (different session)
→ Reapply within 24 hours after every rainfall
→ NOT for drip — clay particles block emitters
```

# EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

## Emergency 1 — Bacterial Blight (Dark Spots on Leaves/Fruit)
```
Same day spray (1000L):
ALV 100L + TRM 60L + GGE 60L → fill 1000L
Apply morning AND evening

Next day drip (200L drum):
PLF 10L + VMW 20L + TNAM 4L → fill 166L

Apply ALV gel undiluted directly on each lesion
Continue blight spray every 2 days until spots stop appearing
After any rain: Spray ALV + TRM within 6 hours
```

## Emergency 2 — Fusarium Wilt (Branch Suddenly Wilts)
```
Immediately:
→ Cut wilting branch 30cm below wilt point
→ Apply TRCH paste + ALV gel on cut surface

Same day drip (200L drum):
PLF 20L + TRCH 20L + TNAM 4L → fill 156L
→ Repeat every day for 5 days

Day 7: VAM 10L + GRK 10L + TNAM 4L → fill 176L
Alternate TRCH and PLF drip every 3 days for 30 days
```

## Emergency 3 — Nalli (Heavy Aphids/Thrips/Mealybug)
```
Day 1 morning: LRK 50L → fill 950L → spray full tree (ALONE)
Day 1 evening: GGE 50L + ALV 50L + TNAM 30L → fill 1000L
Day 2 morning: PKA 40L + ALV 50L → fill 1000L
Day 3 morning: LRK 50L again (double dose)
Continue LRK every 5 days until zero

Drip: NWDC 8L + PLF 6L + TNAM 4L → fill 182L (soil stage)
```

## Emergency 4 — Fruit Borer (Bored Fruit Found)
```
Immediately: Remove and destroy ALL bored fruit
KLN double coat: 8kg KLN in 1000L → spray all fruit
Next day: LRK 50L → 950L (double dose) — evening spray on fruit
Every 3 days: LRK 30L → 970L spray until season ends
Check pheromone traps: If >8 moths/week = emergency KLN + LRK every 3 days
```

## Emergency 5 — Fruit Cracking
```
Cause: Uneven irrigation + Ca deficiency
Immediate spray: BTM 50L + ALV 50L → fill 1000L on all fruit
Drip: Increase MOE to 8L + BTM 4L + TNAM 4L in 200L drum
Reduce irrigation for 3 days → restart gradually
Never stop irrigation suddenly in P7–P10
```

## Emergency 6 — Extreme Heat (>42°C)
```
At 4 AM: Full drip session (normal)
At 5 AM: CNW 100L + SAG 2.5L → fill 1000L spray (full canopy)
Ensure: KLN double coat on all fruit (white coating = reflects heat)
Ensure: Mulch 6 inches thick around each tree
Never spray between 9 AM and 4 PM during 42°C+ days
```
