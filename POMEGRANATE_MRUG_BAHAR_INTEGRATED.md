## ❓ SCENARIO 1: First water given, but FLOWERING DID NOT COME

```
WHY THIS HAPPENS:
1. Insufficient stress → tree not fully dormant → no bud trigger
2. Not enough leaf drop → tree still "comfortable" → won't flower
3. Excess nitrogen in soil → vegetative growth instead of flowering
4. Improper/late pruning → no new flowering wood
5. Sudden heavy rain right after first irrigation → tree confused

DIAGNOSIS (wait 15-20 days after first irrigation):
→ If new LEAF flush only, no flower buds = bahar failed
→ If some flowers but very few = partial bahar

SOLUTION — RE-INDUCE BAHAR:

Step 1 — STOP irrigation again immediately (10-15 days re-stress)
Step 2 — FORCE DEFOLIATION (remove leaves to reset tree):
    ORGANIC method:
    → Spray Buttermilk 5% (BTM 50L in 1000L) — stress spray
    → OR spray potassium-rich WAE 5% to shock into dormancy
    CHEMICAL method (faster, if organic slow):
    → Ethephon (Ethrel) 39% @ 2ml/L = defoliation spray
      (2000ml Ethephon in 1000L) → leaves drop in 5-7 days
    → OR Urea 10% spray (10kg urea in 100L) forces leaf drop

Step 3 — During re-stress, apply PHOSPHORUS shock:
    ORGANIC: Nano DAP 3ml/L spray (3L in 1000L) — P triggers flowering
    → OR bone meal / rock phosphate around root zone
    CHEMICAL: 00:52:34 or 12:61:00 (mono potassium phosphate) foliar 5g/L
    → High P + High K + Zero N = strongest flowering trigger

Step 4 — RESUME irrigation after 10-15 days re-stress:
    → First irrigation: light dose + Nano DAP in drip
    → Flowering should come in 15-20 days

Step 5 — Support flowering:
    → Boron spray (WAE 3%) — pollen readiness
    → Reduce ALL nitrogen — N delays flowering

PREVENTION NEXT TIME:
→ Ensure 45-60 day complete stress (deep leaf drop before irrigation)
→ Prune properly during stress (removes old wood, forces new flowering wood)
→ Zero nitrogen for 30 days before intended flowering
→ Check soil is fully dry and cracking before first irrigation
```

## ❓ SCENARIO 2: HEAVY RAIN during FLOWERING (June-July)

```
PROBLEMS RAIN CAUSES AT FLOWERING:
1. Pollen washed away → no pollination → fruit doesn't set
2. Flower drop increases → fewer fruits
3. Bacterial blight enters through wet flower tissue → Telya starts
4. Boron leached from soil → poor pollen tube growth
5. High humidity → fungal infection of flowers

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:

BEFORE predicted rain (if forecast available):
→ PREVENTIVE bactericide spray:
   Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5g/L (2.5kg in 1000L)
   → Protects flowers and young tissue from blight entry
→ Ensure drainage channels are open

DURING rain:
→ Do NOTHING — never spray in rain (wastes input, spreads disease)
→ Just monitor drainage

AFTER rain stops (within 6-12 hours, first dry window):
→ Bactericide + fungicide spray:
   Copper Oxychloride 2.5g/L + Streptocycline 0.5g/L (0.5kg in 1000L)
   → This is the standard blight knockdown
→ Next dry day: Boron spray (WAE 3% neutralized) — restore pollen ability
→ Calcium spray (BTM 3% on flowers/tiny fruit) — hold fruit set
→ Anti-drop hormone: Coconut water 10% (CNW 100L) OR
   Chemical NAA (Planofix) @ 2ml per 4.5L = reduces flower/fruit drop

FRUIT SET RECOVERY:
→ If most flowers dropped: tree will produce second flush of flowers
→ Support second flush with Boron + P (Nano DAP) + reduce N
→ Multiple flowering flushes is normal in monsoon — manage each

ORGANIC-CHEMICAL BALANCE HERE:
→ Blight prevention: CHEMICAL (copper) is essential in wet flowering
→ Fruit set + drop: ORGANIC (CNW, Boron, Ca) works well
→ Alternate: chemical for disease, organic for nutrition/hormone
```

## ❓ SCENARIO 3: CONTINUOUS RAIN 3-4 DAYS in MID-FRUIT (no waterlogging)

```
THIS IS THE MOST DANGEROUS SCENARIO FOR BLIGHT

PROBLEMS:
1. Bacterial blight EXPLODES — leaves, stem, fruit get oily black spots
2. Fungal fruit spots (Anthracnose, Cercospora, Alternaria)
3. Fruit cracking when sun returns after rain (sudden water uptake)
4. Calcium washing → weak peel → more cracking
5. Fruit rot at calyx end

IMMEDIATE ACTION PLAN:

DAY BEFORE RAIN (if forecast):
→ PREVENTIVE spray (protectant that rain won't fully wash):
   Copper Hydroxide 53.8% @ 2g/L (2kg in 1000L)
   OR Bordeaux mixture 1% (copper sulfate 1kg + lime 1kg in 100L → scale to 10kg+10kg in 1000L)
   → Copper forms protective film, works even through light rain
→ KLN kaolin 5kg in 1000L on fruit (extra physical barrier)

DURING 3-4 day rain:
→ No spraying possible (continuous rain)
→ Check drainage every day — even without waterlogging, standing wet is bad
→ Walk orchard — mark any tree showing black spots for priority treatment

FIRST DRY WINDOW after rain (critical — act within 12 hours):
→ Bactericide knockdown:
   Copper Oxychloride 2.5g/L + Streptocycline 0.5g/L
   OR Bronopol (Bacterinashak) 0.5g/L — very effective for Telya
→ Fungicide for fruit spots:
   Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5g/L (add to copper spray — compatible)
   OR Chlorothalonil 2g/L
→ Remove and DESTROY any blighted fruit/leaves immediately (bury away from farm)

NEXT 2-3 DAYS after knockdown:
→ Calcium spray to prevent cracking: BTM 3% + Calcium chloride 0.5% (chemical Ca)
→ Do NOT let soil suddenly dry — keep even moisture (prevents cracking)
→ Kaolin reapply on fruit

FRUIT CRACKING PREVENTION (key for size + quality):
→ Cracking happens when dry roots suddenly get water OR rain after dry
→ Keep soil moisture EVEN — light drip even during monsoon gaps
→ Calcium + Boron + Silica strengthen peel:
   Organic: BTM (Ca) + WAE (B) + BSJ/PKA (Si)
   Chemical: Calcium chloride 0.5% + Borax 0.2% spray
→ Potassium regulates water movement: MOE high dose in drip

ORGANIC-CHEMICAL BALANCE:
→ 3-4 day continuous rain = RED ALERT = chemical priority
→ Copper + antibiotic + fungicide combination is necessary
→ After knockdown → resume organic (Ca, B, Si, K) for peel + quality
→ This scenario: 70% chemical, 30% organic during the crisis week
```

```
   → Last chemical spray: mid-November (if December harvest)
   → After that: ONLY organic (ALV + TRM + GGE) for blight

   → BEST solution: FRUIT BAGGING (butter paper or newspaper bags)

E. CRACKING (NE monsoon):
   → Even soil moisture (light drip between rains)
   → Calcium + Boron + Silica (organic + chemical Ca if needed)
   → Kaolin reduces surface water absorption

PRE-HARVEST CLEAN PHASE (last 30-40 days):
→ STOP all chemicals (residue-free for market + safety)
→ Only organic: ALV, TRM, GGE for blight; MOE, BTM for quality
→ This ensures clean, chemical-residue-free premium fruit
```

```
→ 100% ORGANIC ONLY — ZERO chemicals

→ ALV + TRM + GGE for any late blight
```

# SECTION 5 — ⚠️ CRITICAL: ORGANIC-CHEMICAL COMPATIBILITY
## What you CAN and CANNOT mix

```
🔴 NEVER MIX — WILL DESTROY EACH OTHER:

Copper compounds (Bordeaux, COC, Cu-hydroxide) are ALKALINE:
❌ Never mix with MOE (acid) — reaction, both wasted
❌ Never mix with TNAM/tamarind (acid) — neutralizes
❌ Never mix with Buttermilk (acid)
❌ Never mix with any biological (TRCH, PLF, NPKD, EJEV) — copper kills bacteria/fungi

All Fungicides + Bactericides + Antibiotics:
❌ Never mix with Trichoderma (TRCH) — kills the fungus
❌ Never mix with Pseudomonas (PLF) — kills the bacteria
❌ Never mix with any live biological input
❌ Never drip biologicals for 7-10 days after soil fungicide drench
```

```
✅ CAN MIX SAFELY:

Chemical + Chemical (compatible pairs):
✅ Copper Oxychloride + Mancozeb (both protectants)
✅ Copper Oxychloride + Streptocycline (standard blight combo)
✅ Insecticide + Fungicide (most combinations — check label)
✅ Emamectin + Mancozeb

Chemical + Organic (safe combinations):
✅ Any chemical + KLN (Kaolin clay) — clay is inert
✅ Copper + Kaolin — enhances coverage
✅ Insecticides + Kaolin
✅ Fungicides + SAG (Sagarika seaweed) — usually compatible (test small first)

Organic that goes AFTER chemical (not mixed, separate day):
✅ ALV, TRM, GGE — blight organics — use on non-chemical days
✅ MOE, BTM, TNAM — nutrition — separate day from copper
```

```
✅ THE INTEGRATED RHYTHM (how to alternate):

DAY 1 (Chemical disease day):
→ Copper Oxychloride 2.5g/L + Streptocycline 0.5g/L + Mancozeb 2.5g/L

DAY 4 (Organic nutrition day):

DAY 7 (Organic soil biology day):
```
